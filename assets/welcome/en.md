# Welcome to SS Writer

SS Writer is a Markdown and text editor. The file on disk is the truth: SS Writer never writes
anything of its own into your documents.

## Writing

- Type Markdown and it is rendered as you write. The markers appear when the caret touches them.
- **Bold** is `Ctrl+B`, *italic* is `Ctrl+I`, and `Ctrl+K` makes a link.
- Press `Ctrl+Shift+P` to find every command.

## Files

- Changes are saved automatically a second after you stop typing.
- Open a folder with `Ctrl+Shift+O` to see its files in the sidebar.
- Word, ODT, RTF and DOC files open as Markdown, with a list of what could not be converted.
